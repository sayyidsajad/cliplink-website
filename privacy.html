Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026. Operator & data controller: Sayyid Sajad (individual, India).

Plain-language summary: ClipLink is designed data-minimal — and since v0.4 your shared files are never stored by us at all: they live in your own Google Drive, and the automated cleanup deletes that copy when the link expires or is revoked. The service keeps a small amount of share metadata for about 7 days to keep the service running and to fight abuse, plus an encrypted-at-rest Google refresh token that lets it create those files in your Drive. There are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising, no profiling, and no sale of data. Ever.

1. Who this applies to

This policy covers the hosted Service at api.cliplink.workers.dev and the public website. If you self-host your own backend, you are the controller of that deployment and should review backend/src/* (fully auditable) plus your own providers' policies.

3. What we deliberately do not do

  • No content monitoring: uploads are not scanned, reviewed, or classified by humans or ML. Link access requires the unguessable token; even the Operator does not browse stored files.
  • No accounts or profiling: there is nothing to identify you with, by design — a random install UUID is regenerated on request and never cross-referenced with identity providers.
  • No cookies or trackers on the website, share pages, or API.
  • No sale/sharing of data. No data brokers. Ever.

4. Third-party processors

The metadata Service is hosted by Cloudflare (Workers, D1 database — global edge network); data transits and is processed in their globally distributed data centres, which may include servers outside your country. Cloudflare is committed to strong security; see the Cloudflare Trust Hub.

Your shared files are not stored by us. Since v0.4 they live in your own Google Drive, under the access you grant. From the Service's perspective, Google processes that file content as your provider (under Google's Terms you already have), not as our sub-processor — we act on your instruction to create a file for a share, and to delete it when the share expires or is revoked. If that deletion cannot happen cleanly (e.g. a Google outage), it is retried automatically until it succeeds. See Google's Privacy Policy for how Google handles your data.

Historically (v0.3, until 25 Oct 2026) file bytes were stored encrypted at rest with Supabase (Supabase privacy policy); all such rows have expired and are purged within seven days of that date.

No other third party receives from us anything about your shares.

5. Security

Transport is HTTPS/TLS-only. File uploads stream through the Worker straight into your Drive using resumable chunked transfers; downloads are brokered through the Worker so that expiry and revocation checks always stay authoritative. Your Google refresh token is stored encrypted with AES-GCM, and the OAuth flow uses PKCE; the app never transmits any token. The app declines to upload obviously sensitive local files (private keys, .env files, wallets). Links are random tokens with no dictionary words or look-alike characters. Revocation takes effect immediately and always removes the file. See the project's SECURITY.md for the full posture and how to report vulnerabilities.

6. Your rights

Because we hold so little, requests about "your data" are simple: metadata rows are anonymous unless you still hold the token/revoke token. If you want proof of deletion of a file you shared, revoke the link from the app (or the recipient can simply let it expire) — the copy in your Drive is deleted by the cleanup job within the cron cycle. You can also end all Drive-related processing at any time by restoring control to yourself: remove ClipLink's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions — after that the Service can no longer create or delete files in your Drive, and anything not yet expired remains under your control; e-mail us at the address below to purge the sealed connection record.

If you are located in the EU/EEA/UK: although we believe no personally identifying data is processed, on request we will attempt to action erasure, access, and objection requests by token; you also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

If you are located in India: under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask us (a) what data about you exists, (b) correction/completion, (c) erasure of data not required by law, and (d) to nominate a representative in case of your death/incapacity. Our lawful basis for the 7-day metadata is legitimate use (service integrity and abuse prevention) and consent (you chose to share the file).

To exercise any of the above: e-mail sayyidsajada4s@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy request", or contact via the project's GitHub issues. The public issue tracker is not the place for personal data — say only "privacy request" there and we will respond privately by e-mail.

7. Children

The Service is not intended for anyone under 18. Because there is no registration or age gate, we ask that minors do not use the Service, and we remove content reported as involving minors without discretion.

8. Data breach response

In the unlikely event of an incident with personal-data impact, affected users will be notified through this website and the GitHub repository within 72 hours, and India's CERT-In reporting duties (as defined by the April 2022 CERT-In directions, where they apply to equipment/service operators) will be honoured. Note that even a successful attacker could obtain only the ephemeral rows described in §2 — no identity data exists in plaintext anywhere in the system, and Drive refresh tokens are unreadable without the operator-held sealing key.

9. Changes

Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date and announced via the GitHub repository. Continued use after a change means acceptance.
