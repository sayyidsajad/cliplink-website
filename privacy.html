Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026. Operator & data controller: Sayyid Sajad (individual, India).

Plain-language summary: ClipLink is designed data-minimal. Shared files are deleted automatically when the link expires. The service keeps a small amount of share metadata for about 7 days to keep the service running and to fight abuse. There are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising, no profiling, and no sale of data. Ever.

1. Who this applies to

This policy covers the hosted Service at api.cliplink.workers.dev and the public website. If you self-host your own backend, you are the controller of that deployment and should review backend/src/* (fully auditable) plus your own providers' policies.

No names. No e-mail addresses. No phone numbers. No cookies. No other logs. No location data beyond the raw IP above. No advertising or analytics of any kind.

3. What we deliberately do not do

  • No content monitoring: uploads are not scanned, reviewed, or classified by humans or ML. Link access requires the unguessable token; even the Operator does not browse stored files.
  • No accounts or profiling: there is nothing to identify you with, by design — a random install UUID is regenerated on request and never cross-referenced with identity providers.
  • No cookies or trackers on the website, share pages, or API.
  • No sale/sharing of data. No data brokers. Ever.

4. Third-party processors

The Service rests on infrastructure hosted by (i) Cloudflare (Workers, D1 database — global edge network) and (ii) Supabase (Postgres and encrypted object storage). Files and metadata physically transit and are processed in their globally distributed data centres, which may include servers outside your country. Both providers are contractually committed to strong security; see the Cloudflare Trust Hub and Supabase privacy policy. No other third party receives from us anything about your shares.

5. Security

Transport is HTTPS/TLS-only. Stored object access is private and brokered with short-lived signed URLs. The app declines to upload obviously sensitive local files (private keys, .env files, wallets). Links are random tokens with no dictionary words or look-alike characters. Revocation takes effect immediately, and no data persists unaccountably — see the project's SECURITY.md for the full posture and how to report vulnerabilities.

6. Your rights

Because we hold so little, requests about "your data" are simple: metadata rows are anonymous unless you still hold the token/revoke token. If you want proof of deletion of a file you shared, revoke the link from the app (or the recipient can simply let it expire) — then the file bytes are deleted by the cleanup job within the cron cycle.

If you are located in the EU/EEA/UK: although we believe no personally identifying data is processed, on request we will attempt to action erasure, access, and objection requests by token; you also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

If you are located in India: under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask us (a) what data about you exists, (b) correction/completion, (c) erasure of data not required by law, and (d) to nominate a representative in case of your death/incapacity. Our lawful basis for the 7-day metadata is legitimate use (service integrity and abuse prevention) and consent (you chose to share the file).

To exercise any of the above: contact via the project's GitHub issues (this channel is public; please avoid including personal data in your message — say "privacy request" and we will respond outside the public issue).

7. Children

The Service is not intended for anyone under 18. Because there is no registration or age gate, we ask that minors do not use the Service, and we remove content reported as involving minors without discretion.

8. Data breach response

In the unlikely event of an incident with personal-data impact, affected users will be notified through this website and the GitHub repository within 72 hours, and India's CERT-In reporting duties (as defined by the April 2022 CERT-In directions, where they apply to equipment/service operators) will be honoured. Note that even a successful attacker could obtain only the ephemeral rows described in §2 — no identity data exists anywhere in the system.

9. Changes

Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date and announced via the GitHub repository. Continued use after a change means acceptance.
